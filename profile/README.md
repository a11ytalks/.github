# A11yTalks

> Virtual meet‑up featuring speakers & conversations around digital accessibility.
> 
_Founded in 2017, A11yTalks is a commnity-driven initiative bringing together designers, developers, testers, advocates, and people with disabilities (PwD) to learn, share, and elevate accessibility in digital products._

---

## 🌟 Mission

To create an open, welcoming, and psychologically safe space where people can learn about, discuss, and champion digital accessibility — while amplifying voices from PwD, BIPOC, LGBTQIA+, women, and other underrepresented groups in tech.

---

## 💡 What We Do

🎤 Host virtual talks, panels, and conversations  
✨ Elevate both emerging and established accessibility voices  
🧪 Share practical, actionable accessibility techniques  
🤝 Connect people across roles, regions, and lived experiences  
📚 Archive recorded talks with updated captions & transcripts  
🎓 Provide free educational resources

## 🤝 Sponsorship & Support

With your help, we can create the most accessible and fun space possible to learn, share, and grow digital accessibility knowledge in our community, through our mission and values.

Since its inception, A11yTalks has hosted a variety of live talks and panels covering topics related to digital accessibility for beginners, developers, and creatives. All videos are streamed and then archived on the A11yTalks YouTube channel with updated captions.

Our content is available to the public as a free resource for digital accessibility education, as well as continuing education credits for certified International Association of Accessibility Professionals (IAAP).
